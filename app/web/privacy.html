Introduction

Welcome to Chess Master ("the App," "we," "us," "our," or "the Company"). We respect your privacy and are committed to transparent data practices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Chess Master mobile application.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Username (required)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)
  • Account creation date and profile picture (optional)
  • Two-factor authentication preferences

1.2 Device Information

  • Device identifier (hashed for authentication robustness)
  • Operating system and version
  • App version and build number
  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Unique advertising identifier (if enabled)

1.3 Game & Activity Data

  • Complete move history for all games
  • Game outcomes (win/loss/draw)
  • Player ratings and ELO score
  • Puzzle completion records
  • Time spent in game modes
  • Feature usage patterns
  • In-app achievements and progress
  • Chat messages (if multiplayer feature)

1.4 User-Generated Content

  • Game board screenshots (when user uploads for analysis)
  • Game annotations and notes
  • Custom piece sets or themes you create

1.5 Connectivity Data

  • Network status (WiFi vs. cellular)
  • Connection type and signal strength (for optimization only)
  • Approximate location via IP address (for latency optimization, not stored)

2. How We Use Your Data

  1. Provide Game Services: Enable multiplayer gameplay, compute ratings, sync games across devices
  2. Account Security: Authenticate logins, detect fraudulent access, enforce two-factor authentication
  3. AI Analysis: Power move suggestions, opponent matching, and post-game tactical breakdowns
  4. Personalization: Recommend difficulty levels, suggest matching opponents, track progress
  5. Improvement: Analyze gameplay patterns, generate personalized coaching tips
  6. App Optimization: Diagnose crashes, improve UI/UX, optimize performance
  7. Legal Compliance: Respond to legal requests, enforce Terms of Service, prevent abuse
  8. Fraud Prevention: Detect cheating (engine assistance), account takeovers, rating manipulation

Data Type Legal Basis

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Where We Store Data

  • Backend: Cloudflare Workers D1 database (UK data center)
  • On Your Device: Hive database (local SQLite) encrypted with Hive secure storage
  • Cache: User profile images cached locally on device
  • No cloud backup: Offline game history remains on device only

4.2 Security Measures

  • ✅ Encryption in Transit: All API calls use HTTPS/TLS 1.3
  • ✅ Encryption at Rest: Database encrypted with AES-256
  • ✅ Password Hashing: bcrypt with salt (never stored in plain text)
  • ✅ Device ID: SHA-256 hashed, never transmitted raw
  • ✅ Two-Factor Authentication: TOTP-based (optional)
  • ✅ Rate Limiting: Protection against brute-force attacks
  • ✅ Regular Audits: Security code reviews, penetration testing annually

4.3 Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
Account information Until account deletion
Game history 2 years; older games archived
Device ID Until account deletion or 5 years of inactivity
Crash logs 30 days
Chat/abuse reports 1 year (legal compliance)

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We Share Data With:

  • Cloudflare Workers: Backend API host (GDPR-compliant DPA signed)
  • Cloudflare D1: Database provider (SOC 2 Type II certified)
  • No advertising networks — Chess Master is ad-free
  • No data brokers — We never sell user data
  • Google Play Services: Analytics & crash reporting (if enabled)

We Do NOT Share Data With:

  • ❌ Social media platforms
  • ❌ Marketing services
  • ❌ Third-party analytics (except optional Play Services)
  • ❌ Government agencies (except legal compulsion with warrant)

Legal Requests

We may disclose information if required by law (court order, subpoena, government request) or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or user safety. We will notify you of legal requests unless prohibited by law.

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access & Portability

You can export all your data anytime:

  1. Open Chess Master app
  2. Settings → Account → Privacy
  3. Select "Export My Data"
  4. Receive JSON file with all personal data, game history, ratings

6.2 Deletion Rights

You can permanently delete your account:

  1. Settings → Account → Delete Account
  2. Confirm identity (email verification or 2FA)
  3. All personal data deleted within 30 days
  4. Game history anonymized (preserved for rating integrity)

Note: Anonymized game records are retained (you cannot be identified) because removing them would corrupt the rating system for all players.

6.3 Correction & Updates

You can update your profile anytime:

  • Username (change once every 30 days)
  • Email address
  • Profile picture
  • Privacy settings

6.4 Right to Object

You can opt out of:

  • Push notifications (Settings → Notifications)
  • Analytics tracking (Settings → Privacy → Analytics)
  • Personalized recommendations (Settings → Privacy → Personalization)

Note: You cannot opt out of essential data collection (account info, game data) as it's necessary to provide the service.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Chess Master is not marketed to children under 13.

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
  • If you are under 13, please have a parent/guardian create your account
  • If we discover a child's account without parental consent, we will delete it immediately
  • Parental accounts can monitor and restrict child accounts (coming Q2 2026)
If you believe we have collected data from a child without consent, contact us immediately: privacy@chessmaster.app

8. International Data Transfers

Chess Master operates globally, and your data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws than your country.

For EU Users (GDPR): Data transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with Cloudflare. View Cloudflare's GDPR compliance.

For California Users (CCPA): You have the right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale (we don't sell data). View CCPA notice.
